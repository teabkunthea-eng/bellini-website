Our Story

A Little Corner of Italy
in Phnom Penh

Bellini was born from a simple idea — bring real Italian bistro culture to Cambodia. Three Italians, one lush garden, and an unwavering commitment to the food, drinks, and atmosphere they grew up with.

From homemade limoncello and slow-cooked sauces to seasonal concerts under the stars, every evening at Bellini is a little slice of Italy in the heart of BKK1.

Live Events

Concerts Under
the Stars

Every dry season, Bellini transforms into Phnom Penh's most atmospheric concert venue. Jazz concerts, opera nights, classical piano evenings, and cultural performances — all in the open-air garden.

Concert season runs every November through April.
