Bellini Concert Season

Music in the Garden

Every dry season, Bellini transforms its garden into one of Phnom Penh's most atmospheric concert venues. Jazz concerts, opera nights, classical piano evenings, and cultural performances — all under the open sky, surrounded by tropical greenery, good food, and great drinks.

Authentic Italian cuisine, craft cocktails, and live concerts in a lush open-air garden in the heart of BKK1, Phnom Penh.
